OUR HERITAGE

Providing Healthcare with Uncompromising Quality

Founded in Jordan in 1975, Dar Aldawa has grown from a regional pioneer to a trusted global pharmaceutical manufacturer. Guided by our corporate tagline "Trusted Quality", we commit our extensive scientific capabilities to the development and delivery of world-class therapeutic solutions.

"Our mission isn't just about manufacturing medicine; it is about creating a healthier world through accessible science."

— Board of Directors, Dar Aldawa
